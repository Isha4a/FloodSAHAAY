The signal path / FloodSAHAAY

Read the water
before it arrives.

FloodSAHAAY is a six-layer response system: it senses the conditions around you, explains the signal, and gives you a clear next step.

01 location→02 telemetry→03 risk→04 map→05 response
01
Sense

Choose the field.

Search a city, area, or pincode, drop a map pin, or share your GPS position.

02
Read

Watch live conditions.

Rainfall, temperature, humidity, wind, and soil moisture arrive from live weather telemetry.

03
Score

Understand the signal.

The 0–100 score combines weather inputs with regional hydrological vulnerability.

04
See

Map the move.

See your risk area, nearby facilities, shelter capacity, and routes on an interactive map.

05
Connect

Keep your circle close.

Store emergency contacts and invite people you trust into your safety circle.

06
Act

Send a signal.

Hold SOS for a broadcast, or use the simulated nearby mesh layer when a mobile network is unavailable.
