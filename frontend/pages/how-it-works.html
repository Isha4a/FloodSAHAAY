HOW IT WORKS

How FloodSAHAAY Protects Communities

FloodSAHAAY uses live weather data, soil moisture tracking, and automatic emergency alerts to warn you about floods in your area.

📍 Location → 📡 Weather Data → 📊 Risk Calculator → 🗺️ Interactive Map → 🚨 Emergency Alert
1

Select & Monitor Location

Search for any city or area. FloodSAHAAY pinpoints your location to track flood risk right where you are.

2

Live Weather Updates

We pull current rainfall, temperature, wind, and soil moisture data from live weather sources — updated every few seconds.

3

Flood Risk Score

We combine how much it's raining (45%), how fast it's raining (30%), and how soaked the ground is (25%) into a simple 0–100 score: Low, Moderate, High, or Critical.

4

Live Flood Map

The map shows danger zones, nearby shelters, and how full each shelter is — all in real time.

5

Alerts & Warning Notices

When rain gets dangerously heavy, you'll get a flood alert and directions to the nearest safe shelter.

6

Emergency Contact & SOS Network

Press and hold the SOS button to instantly share your live location with your emergency contacts.
