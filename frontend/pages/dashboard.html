OFFLINE-READY RECOVERY GUIDE

After the Flood: Health & Safety Protocols

Essential steps to prevent disease outbreaks, electrical shocks, and contamination after waters recede.

🦠

Waterborne Disease Prevention

Watch for fever, vomiting, and diarrhea (cholera, typhoid, leptospirosis). Avoid wading in stagnant puddles with open cuts or scratches.

🚰

Safe Drinking Water

Boil water vigorously for at least 1 minute before drinking or cooking. If boiling is impossible, treat with chlorine/halogen tablets according to packet instructions.

🍞

Food Safety & Disposal

Discard all food that came into contact with floodwater, including canned items with bulging, damaged, or rusted seams.

⚡

Electrical & Gas Hazard Safety

Never touch electrical appliances or meters while standing in water. Have an electrician inspect flooded wiring before flipping main circuit breakers.

🐍

Snake Bites & Wound Care

Floods displace reptiles and insects. Wear sturdy rubber boots and gloves when clearing debris. In case of snake bite, immobilize limb and call 108 immediately.
