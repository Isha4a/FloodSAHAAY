CRITICAL FLOOD HAZARD

Snake Bite Emergency Management Protocol

🚨 Call Ambulance 108

Why Snake Bites Surge After Floods: Floodwaters submerge ground burrows, driving snakes (Cobras, Russell's Vipers, Kraits) to take refuge in dry huts, rooftops, furniture, bedding, and tree branches. Anti-Snake Venom (ASV) administered in a hospital is the ONLY cure.

✅ WHAT TO DO IMMEDIATELY (Do's)

  • Keep the victim 100% calm and immobile: Any movement, panic, or walking increases heart rate and speeds up venom dissemination through the lymphatic system.
  • Immobilize the bitten limb: Splint the arm or leg with a firm stick or rolled cardboard and a broad, snug bandage. Prevent any joint from bending.
  • Keep the bite BELOW heart level: Prevents rapid venous rush toward vital organs.
  • Remove tight items immediately: Rings, bracelets, watches, anklets, and shoes MUST come off before swelling becomes severe.
  • Remember snake appearance (if safe): Note color, head shape, and stripe pattern to tell doctors. Do NOT try to catch or kill the snake.
  • Transport immediately to hospital: Keep patient lying flat on their side (Recovery Position) during transport.

❌ WHAT NEVER TO DO (Deadly Myths)

  • DO NOT cut, slash, or make incisions: Cutting through bite punctures damages nerves, causes uncontrollable arterial bleeding, and worsens infection.
  • DO NOT suck venom with mouth or pump: Venom is absorbed deep in tissue within seconds. Mouth contact can poison the rescuer.
  • DO NOT apply tight tourniquets or ropes: Cutting off arterial blood flow causes severe localized tissue death (gangrene) and limb amputation.
  • DO NOT apply ice, electricity, or chemicals: Ice causes severe frostbite necrosis; electric shocks can cause cardiac arrest.
  • DO NOT visit faith healers (Jhad-phook): Wastes the "Golden Hour" (first 60–90 mins) when Anti-Snake Venom is most effective.
  • DO NOT give aspirin, pain pills, or alcohol: Aspirin thins blood and increases internal bleeding from viper bites.

Flood Wound Care & Tetanus

Infection & Leptospirosis Control

Floodwater contains raw sewage, soil bacteria, and leptospira organisms from rodent urine. Open cuts exposed to floodwaters carry life-threatening infection risks.

Step-by-step cleaning:
1. Flush wound thoroughly with clean boiled/bottled water and soap.
2. Apply Povidone-Iodine (Betadine 10%) or chlorhexidine antiseptic.
3. Cover with sterile gauze; keep elevated and dry.
4. Tetanus Toxoid (TT) Warning: If wound was caused by rusty metal, submerged tin, or nails, get a TT injection within 48 hours.
⚠️ Watch for "Rat Fever" (Leptospirosis): High fever, yellowing eyes (jaundice), red eyes, and severe calf muscle pain.
🫀

Drowning Resuscitation & CPR

Emergency Airway Revival

When a person is pulled unconscious from floodwater, brain survival depends on immediate oxygenation within 3–4 minutes.

CPR Protocol:
1. Check responsiveness: Tap shoulders and shout. Check breathing for 10 seconds.
2. Hands-Only CPR: Place hands in center of victim's chest. Press hard and fast (100–120 compressions/min, 2 inches deep).
3. If trained in breaths: Give 30 compressions followed by 2 gentle rescue breaths (head tilt, chin lift).
4. Recovery Position: If breathing resumes, turn victim onto their side with upper knee bent to prevent choking on vomited water.
🚨 Never attempt to drain water from the stomach with abdominal thrusts—it causes regurgitation into lungs.
💧

Safe Drinking Water & DIY ORS

Prevent Cholera, Typhoid & Dehydration

Municipal pipes and groundwater wells become heavily contaminated during floods. Never drink unfiltered or unboiled tap water.

3 Purification Methods:
• Vigorous Boiling: Rolling boil for at least 1–3 minutes.
• Halazone/Chlorine Tablets: 1 tablet per 20 Liters of clear water. Wait 30 minutes before drinking.
• Liquid Chlorine (5%): 2 drops per Liter of clear water.
Life-Saving DIY ORS Formula:
Mix 1 Litre boiled water + 6 teaspoons sugar + 1/2 teaspoon salt. Administer frequently for diarrhea/vomiting.
✓ Safe storage: Store treated water in narrow-mouth, covered containers to prevent recontamination.

Trench Foot & Fungal Skin Care

Prolonged Wet Foot Management

Standing in wet footwear or damp mud for more than 10–12 hours constricts blood vessels, destroying nerve endings and skin tissue (Immersion Foot).

Prevention Guidelines:
• Remove wet shoes and socks as soon as reaching dry shelter.
• Wash feet with clean water and mild soap; dry thoroughly between toes.
• Elevate feet above heart level for 30 minutes to restore circulation.
• Apply antifungal powder (Clotrimazole) or coconut oil to prevent skin cracking.
• Warning signs: Cold, numb, white, or blotchy blue skin requires urgent medical evaluation.
⚡

Electrical & Gas Re-Entry Safety

Prevent Electrocution & Explosions

Submerged electrical wiring and dislodged LPG gas cylinders cause fatal accidents when floodwaters recede.

Re-entry Precautions:
• Never step in water near outlets or appliances: Water conducts current even from partially submerged sockets.
• Switch off main circuit breaker only if the fuse box and your standing area are completely dry.
• Smell for gas (LPG): If you smell rotten eggs/sulfur, DO NOT turn on any light switch, match, or lighter. Evacuate immediately.
• Do not use flooded electronics: Motors, inverters, and refrigerators must be dried and certified by a technician before power-on.
🏚️

Building & Structural Inspection

Avoid Ceiling Collapses & Sinkholes

Receding floodwaters erode foundation soil, causing unexpected wall collapses and sinkholes.

Checklist Before Re-Entering Home:
• Look for horizontal or diagonal cracks in exterior walls.
• Check if roof or ceiling is sagging under absorbed water weight.
• Tap on floor tiles to verify soil beneath hasn't washed away.
• Avoid leaning against boundary walls or waterlogged brick structures.
• Discard all contaminated mattresses, pillows, and soaked upholstered furniture to stop black mold spores.
