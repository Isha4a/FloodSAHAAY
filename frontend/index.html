The signal path

From atmosphere to action.

Every layer has a job: sense, explain, show, connect, and help you move.

01 / SENSE

Choose a place

Search a city, drop a pin, or use your GPS to start a local monitoring window.

02 / READ

Watch the sky

Live rainfall, wind, temperature, humidity, and ground moisture arrive from weather telemetry.

03 / SCORE

Understand risk

A 0–100 signal combines current conditions with regional hydrological vulnerability.

04 / SEE

Map the move

Use maps to see your area, nearby shelters, distance, capacity, and a route out.

05 / CONNECT

Keep your circle close

Store trusted contacts and create a safety circle before conditions change.

06 / ACT

Send a signal

Hold SOS for an emergency dispatch, or use the nearby mesh when cellular service drops.
